# Practiced Doctrine: Grounding the Narrator's Condemnation of the Lived Law

## 0. Purpose and Method

This volume presumes [DOCTRINAL-SINS.md](DOCTRINAL-SINS.md) is read first. Its method governs
here, tightened in one respect. Volume I measured the adversary's *creed*; this volume measures
his *law as actually administered* — Quran + mutawatir hadith + the consensus of the classical
schools (the four Sunni madhhabs and the Shi'a), i.e., the doctrine as it walked shod in the
world of 1095-1192 and its juridical scaffolding. This is the owner's correction: when doctrine
is only examined in abstraction, one misses the sins that live in its *practice*.

The discipline is unchanged and restated for completeness:

- **Charge doctrine, not peoples.** The dhimma scribe, the qadi at the apostasy hearing, the
  slave-trader — these are victims and functionaries of a law; the narrator's arrows go to the
  *statute*. Pity souls, condemn propositions.
- **Primary citations both sides.** Quran: [Corpus Coranicum](https://corpuscoranicum.de/en)
  (Cairo 1924 reference). Hadith: [Sunnah.com](https://sunnah.com) canonical numbering.
  Scripture: [Bible Gateway, RSVCE](https://www.biblegateway.com/).
- **The Seven map only where honestly defensible**; otherwise tag *Doctrinal Error*. Forcing a
  mapping produces cheap writing.
- **The mirror stands (Vol. I §4.8).** Every practiced sin named here has a Latin parallel the
  narrator must also indict: Latin slavery, harsh marriage law, oath-perjury. The chronicler's
  censoriousness is credibly universal or it is propaganda.
- **Ban-register from Vol. I §2.2 carries over in full.**

## 1. The Dhimma and the Jizya: Humiliation as Statute

- **Islamic text:** "Fight those who do not believe in Allah… [from among] those who were given
  the Scripture, until they give the jizyah willingly while they are *ṣāghirūn* — humbled/brought
  low" ([Q 9:29](https://corpuscoranicum.de/)). The final clause is not commentary gloss but
  verse: the subjection, not merely the tax, is legislated. The classical elaboration — the
  so-called Pact of Umar (attributed variously to Umar I or Umar II, held authoritative in the
  medieval dhimmi regime) — enumerated enforced conditions: no new churches may be built and old
  ones repaired only by consent; no church may exceed Muslim houses in height; the ghiyār
  (distinctive dress) marks the dhimmi; he must rise from his seat for a Muslim; no public
  display of wine, crosses, or scripture; no cavalry arms; no marrying Muslim women
  ([A.S. Tritton, *The Caliphs and Their Non-Muslim Subjects*, 1930;
  overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pact_of_Umar)). Ibn Qayyim al-Jawziyya's *Aḥkām ahl
  al-dhimma* (14th c., systematizing the classical regime) defends ritual humiliation at the
  collection of the tax itself.
- **Christian text:** "The stranger who sojourns with you shall be to you as the native among
  you, and you shall love him as yourself" ([Leviticus 19:34](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Leviticus+19%3A34&version=RSVCE));
  "I was a stranger and you welcomed me" ([Matthew 25:35](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+25%3A35&version=RSVCE));
  the Good Samaritan, commanded love crossing the deepest polemic line of its day
  ([Luke 10:25-37](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke+10%3A25-37&version=RSVCE)).
  And on the question of *pride in law*: "You pay tithe… and have neglected the weightier
  matters of the law" ([Matthew 23:23](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+23%3A23&version=RSVCE)).
- **The right idiom for the narrator (important):** do not only moralize jizya as personal
  Pride. The Bible has a *canonical register for institutionalized oppression* and the game
  should borrow its cadence: Amos — "they sell the righteous for silver, and the needy for a
  pair of shoes" ([Amos 2:6; 5:11-12](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Amos+2%3A6%3B+5%3A11-12&version=RSVCE));
  Isaiah — "Woe to those who decree iniquitous decrees… to rob the needy of justice"
  ([Isaiah 10:1-2](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Isaiah+10%3A1-2&version=RSVCE));
  Habakkuk — "the law is slacked and justice never goes forth" ([Habakkuk 1:4](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Habakkuk+1%3A4&version=RSVCE)).
  Amos's woe-oracle is precisely *grave, national, legislative* — the perfect voice for
  condemning a statute rather than a soul.
- **Sin-mapping: Pride (defensible, institutional) + Oppression tag.** The ṣaghār clause is
  legislated humiliation — Pride enthroned as jurisprudence, *superbia* with a seal. Map to the
  *decree*, per the Amos idiom. Add a standing game tag **Oppression** (beside Doctrinal Error)
  for legally structured degradation; the Seven alone lack the noun.
- **Mirror:** the Latin Kingdom levied its own discriminatory dues on Muslim peasants, and the
  Franks generally left native Muslim fellahin worse apart from them; serfdom in Europe was a
  caste of unfree birth with its own humiliations. The narrator indicts both (see §9).
- **Narrator logic:** "He taxed the stranger's faith, and wrote the homage of the bowed head
  into a receipt."

## 2. The Apostasy Sword: The Law That Hunts the Conscience

- **Islamic text:** "They wish that you should disbelieve as they disbelieve… If they turn
  their backs, take them and kill them wherever you find them" ([Q 4:89](https://corpuscoranicum.de/)).
  The juridical teeth are the mutawatir sound hadith: "Whoever changes his religion, kill him"
  (*man baddala dīnahu fa'qtulūh*) — [Sahih al-Bukhari 3017, Book of Jihad](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:3017)
  and [Sahih al-Bukhari 6922, Book of Apostates](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6922) (both
  variants verified in this research pass; note Ibn Abbas invokes it approvingly over Ali's
  burning of apostates — the killing itself is the consensus baseline, only the method
  disputed). Sunni fiqh across the four schools sets death as the ruling for the unrepentant
  male apostate (schools differing on the istitāba window of three days and on female
  apostates); the madhhab consensus, not any fringe, is the point.
- **Christian text:** At the great winnowing of the bread-of-life discourse, the Lord lets the
  offended depart: "After this many of his disciples drew back and no longer went about with
  him. Jesus said to the twelve, 'Do you also wish to go away?'" — *no sword is sent after
  them* ([John 6:66-68](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John+6%3A66-68&version=RSVCE));
  "For freedom Christ has set us free" ([Galatians 5:1](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Galatians+5%3A1&version=RSVCE));
  "Whoever holds fast his confession" is commanded, never *compelled* to remain
  ([Hebrews 4:14](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Hebrews+4%3A14&version=RSVCE)).
- **Analysis:** From the Christian side this is the sharpest practical inversion available: the
  same act — changing one's allegiance — is a *door to life* in the Gospel conversion arc
  (Saul-to-Paul) and a capital crime in the hudud. The medieval Christian reader saw it
  immediately: the law guards its fold with the very instrument the Church is forbidden to use
  on consciences. John 6:67 is the standing contradiction — the Shepherd asks, does not
  threaten.
- **Sin-mapping: Wrath (defensible, institutional).** The death sentence on the exiter is
  legislated anger against conscience; the period voice for wrath-with-a-seal is Cain's
  institution ("the earth has opened its mouth to receive your brother's blood"),
  [Genesis 4:10-11](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis+4%3A10-11&version=RSVCE).
- **CONVERSION-mechanic weight (CONVERSION.md §6):** an apostate-under-sentence is the reason
  baptism must be a *decision of consequence*, not a free reroll. A converted archetype who has
  `converted: true` has, in-lore, a writ over his head. This *adds* weight to the loop: the
  Testimonies page pays the emotional price of the man's courage, and the narrator honors it: the
  early Church's word for the persecuted convert was *confessor*.
- **Narrator logic:** "Their law keepeth the fold not as a shepherd but as a jailer; it slayeth
  at the door what the Gospel calleth through it."

## 3. Slavery and Concubinage in Practice

- **Islamic text:** "And [also prohibited to you are all] married women except those your right
  hands possess" ([Q 4:24](https://corpuscoranicum.de/)); "except from their wives or those whom
  their right hands possess — for they are not blameworthy" ([Q 23:5-6](https://corpuscoranicum.de/)).
  The *ma malakat aymānukum* clause is not a tolerated abuse but a licensed estate: concubinage
  by statute. Beyond the text, the practice ran to industrial scale: the trans-Saharan and
  Ottoman trades moved, by the most careful modern estimates, on the order of ten-plus million
  souls across the Islamic centuries; the Zanj revolt in Iraq (869-883) was a slave war of
  African captives on a scale antiquity would have recognized
  (see e.g. [Wikipedia: History of slavery in the Muslim world](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_slavery_in_the_Muslim_world);
  [Wikipedia: Barbary slave trade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbary_slave_trade)).
  Caesarean slavery in al-Andalus, the devshirme of the Christian child-levy (post-period but
  jurisprudentially continuous), and mamluk recruitment from the warp of the Caucasus and the
  East — slaves of conquest made into the realm's elite — all belong to the same lived order.
- **Christian text:** Paul sends Onesimus home to Philemon "no longer as a slave but more than
  a slave, as a beloved brother" ([Philemon 15-16](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Philemon+15-16&version=RSVCE));
  "you were bought with a price; do not become slaves of men" and "so, brethren, in whatever
  state each was called, there let him remain" with the master's conscience undercut from
  within ([1 Corinthians 7:21-24](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1+Corinthians+7%3A21-24&version=RSVCE));
  "There is neither Jew nor Greek, neither slave nor free… for you are all one in Christ Jesus"
  ([Galatians 3:28](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Galatians+3%3A28&version=RSVCE)).
  Historically: the Scripture works against slavery from inside its own society; the
  New-Testament frame never *legislates* concubinage — it politeuetically dissolves it.
- **Analysis and honest mirror:** Scripture's gentleness toward slaves is not abolition by
  statute, and the Crusader states held slaves (Latin estates, Muslim captives sold at Acre and
  Ascalon; Genoese and Venetian traffickers profiting from both directions). The narrator must
  not let Christian gloss over this. The doctrinal difference the game may press: the Quran
  *names and licenses* the concubine; the New Testament *never does* — the harem statute is
  the novel thing, and the Christian verdict on concubinage falls under Vol. I §1.5 (Lust)
  and remains here.
- **Sin-mapping: Greed + Lust (defensible, twinned).** The trade (scale, profit, caravan
  logistics) is Greed; the licensed concubine-bed is Lust. Map both, at the *system* level —
  never at a captive depicted on screen, who in the chronicle voice is *miserabilis*, an object
  of pity.
- **Narrator logic:** "He made a merchandise of the image of God, and then sanctified the
  market with a verse."

## 4. The Practice Toward Women: Halved Witness, Halved Inheritance, the Lash-Verse, the Child Bride

- **Islamic text:** "And get two witnesses from among your men… and if there are not two men,
  then a man and two women… so that if one errs, the other can remind her" ([Q 2:282](https://corpuscoranicum.de/));
  "Allah instructs you concerning your children: for the male, what is equal to the share of
  two females" ([Q 4:11](https://corpuscoranicum.de/)); "And those [wives] from whom you fear
  arrogance — admonish them, then refuse to share their beds, then *wa-ḍribūhunna*" — strike
  them ([Q 4:34](https://corpuscoranicum.de/); classical fiqh qualifies the method — not the
  face, no breaking of bone — but holds the license itself). On marriage age: the Prophet's
  own union with Aisha is attested in the soundest hadith: married at six, consummated at nine
  ([Sahih al-Bukhari 5133](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:5133), verified this pass), and
  [Q 65:4](https://corpuscoranicum.de/) legislates the waiting period for divorcees who "have
  not yet menstruated" — a verse the tafsir tradition itself understands of pre-pubescent
  wives.
- **Christian text:** "Husbands, love your wives, as Christ loved the church and gave himself
  up for her" ([Ephesians 5:25](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ephesians+5%3A25&version=RSVCE));
  "the two shall become one flesh… What therefore God has joined together, let not man put
  asunder" ([Mark 10:6-9](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Mark+10%3A6-9&version=RSVCE));
  and the period's own banner-text for the virtuous woman, a wife and mother reigning in
  commerce and wisdom, "her husband… praised in the gates"
  ([Proverbs 31:10-31](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Proverbs+31&version=RSVCE)).
- **Analysis (honest, two-edged):** The Christian side's own period practice was grim by modern
  measure — canon law's consent theory was real but not uniform in plebe; brutality against
  wives was endemic on both shores and medieval Christian writing idealized the *patient* wife.
  The structural asymmetry the narrator may press: the Gospel's load-beam is the husband's
  *self-giving* ("as Christ gave himself up"); the fiqh's load-beam on the disobedient wife is
  an authorized strike. One structure self-denies; the other self-permits. Both cultures sinned
  against women; one had its violence *as an excrescence against its own text*, the other *as
  a clause of the text itself*.
- **Sin-mapping: Lust (the child-bride precedent and the concubine estate, carried from Vol. I
  §1.5) + Wrath (the strike-clause, defensible as legislated violence) + Pride (the halves of
  witness and inheritance, as a statute of asymmetric dignity).** Honest note: the halves are
  closer to Doctrinal Error — a jurisprudential diminishing — than to any capital sin
  *simpliciter*; tag them **Pride-with-Oppression**, never merely Pride.
- **Narrator logic:** "In their book the daughter is weighed at half a son; in their statute
  the disobedient wife receiveth the rod; and their prophet took a child of nine to his bed,
  and wrote the license into the record of the faithful."

## 5. Oaths and Their Liquidation: The Expiation Loophole and the Hudna Logic

- **Islamic text:** "Allah will not impose blame upon you for what is meaningless in your
  oaths, but He will impose blame upon you for what your hearts have earned… its expiation is
  the feeding of ten needy people" ([Q 5:89](https://corpuscoranicum.de/)); "And Allah has made
  for you from your oaths a dissolution" ([Q 66:2](https://corpuscoranicum.de/)). The classical
  jurisprudence of jihad adds the *hudna*: the truce is a tactical instrument, capped by the
  schools at ten years (some jurists at the Prophet's own Hudaybiyya precedent), and resumed
  war when the imam judges it is broken or ripe; [Q 8:58](https://corpuscoranicum.de/) — "if
  you fear treachery from a people, throw their treaty back to them" — gives the disposal
  formula.
- **Christian text:** "Again you have heard that it was said to those of old, 'You shall not
  swear falsely'… But I say to you, Do not swear at all… Let what you say be simply 'Yes' or
  'No'; anything more of this comes from evil" ([Matthew 5:33-37](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+5%3A33-37&version=RSVCE));
  "When a man vows a vow to the Lord… he shall not break his word; he shall do according to
  all that proceeds out of his mouth" ([Numbers 30:2](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Numbers+30%3A2&version=RSVCE)).
- **Analysis (extension, not duplication, of Vol. I §1.6):** Vol. I treated taqiyya — lying
  under duress for faith — and mapped it to Doctrinal Error. *This* item is practiced: an
  *expiation economy* in which a broken oath has a payable price (feed ten poor, clothe ten,
  or fast three days). The Gospel's logic is the opposite: not that a hedge be built around
  oaths, but that speech be so clean no oath is needed. On truces: hudna-doctrine makes the
  treaty *mortgageable to strategy*; the Christian frame (Numbers 30; Psalm 15:4 — "he that
  sweareth to his own hurt and changeth not") makes the vow *binding against advantage*.
  Note with fairness: the Pact-breaking at Kar Deir/Shaiza 1173 and Raynald of Châtillon's
  caravan violations were Latins practicing the same sin Saladin's jurists *codified*; the
  narrator indicts Raynald by name (Vol. I §4.8 mirror).
- **Sin-mapping: Doctrinal Error (Untruth) with a Wrath hook where the breach is operational
  (hudna-as-ambush).** The expiation economy is not any single capital sin; it is a *legal
  technology* for what the Lord taught men to be incapable of needing.
- **Narrator logic:** "He payeth for his broken word in loaves, and calleth the ledger
  absolution; the Lord taught men a 'Yes' that could not be bought back."

## 6. Fatalism and Piety Without Grace

- **Islamic text:** "Wherever you may be, death will overtake you… Whatever good reaches you is
  from Allah, but whatever evil befalls you is from yourself" ([Q 4:78](https://corpuscoranicum.de/));
  "Allah has set a measure for all things" ([Q 65:3](https://corpuscoranicum.de/)); the classic
  *qadar* hadith corpus anchors the doctrine in divine decree's priority to creation — the Book
  of Destiny cycle (e.g., [Sahih Muslim 2653, Book of Destiny](https://sunnah.com/muslim:2653a),
  the Adam–Moses debate where Adam stumps Moses with "do you blame me for what was decreed
  fifty-thousand-years before my making"), and the Sahih Muslim attestations of the pen drying
  and the Prophethood fit inside the predestined column. Within Islam itself the Ash'ari synthesis (*kasb*, "acquisition") was a famous
  answer to the rawer Jabriyya fatalism — meaning **Muslim scholastics themselves flagged the
  lived drift**. The folk-form is the *inshallah* hollowing into resignation, satirized in the
  *Arabian Nights* register and criticized by medieval Muslim moralists (al-Ghazali's
  *Ihya* itself hammers passive trust-as-neglect).
- **Christian text:** "We know that in everything God works for good with those who love him"
  ([Romans 8:28](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans+8%3A28&version=RSVCE));
  "we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works, which God prepared
  beforehand, that we should walk in them" ([Ephesians 2:10](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ephesians+2%3A10&version=RSVCE)).
  Christian providence is *teleological* (toward good) and *co-worked*; the sharp pole of
  Islamic decree is *bare* (simply "set").
- **Analysis and honesty verdict:** This is the item where honest mapping fails and the prompt
  itself anticipates that. The obverse tradition in Christendom — misread Augustinian
  predestination, the folk-Catholic shrug at "it was fated" — has its own condemnations
  (Trent's canons against stoicizing providence, and every homilist against the shrug).
  *Inshallah* as a phrase is sound piety ("If the Lord wills," James 4:15); the *lived* drift
  into passivity is a species of **Sloth** only in its degenerate social form, and the
  theologians of Islam told their own that. Therefore:
- **Sin-mapping: Doctrinal Error, with a strictly limited Sloth note.** Only where a specific
  character's inaction is *theologically excused passivity* (a boss who refuses rescue of his
  own men because "the pen has dried") may flavor use Sloth-tag, and at that character's
  *abuse*, not at the doctrine of qadar *tout court*.
- **Narrator logic:** "Their book telleth them the pen is dry; and some among them make of that
  dryness a pillow: 'what is written, is written.' But the Lord's Book telleth us we are His
  handiwork, made for the works He set before; and the lazy servant who hid his pound is not
  blamed for his theology but for his field."
- **Mirror:** the narrator rebukes the Latin "quod Deus vult" when it is made a license for
  plunder and an excuse for sloth — the same abuse in Christian dress.

## 7. Zakat and the Public Alms: An Unmappable Item, Honestly So

- **Islamic text:** "If you disclose your charities, it is well; but if you conceal them and
  give them to the poor, it is better for you" ([Q 2:271](https://corpuscoranicum.de/)). Zakat
  itself is not alms-giving in the Christian sense but a *pillar-tax* — a religious due,
  calculated, administered by the state in its ideal form, and a theological duty of social
  hygiene ("take from their wealth a charity to purify them," [Q 9:103](https://corpuscoranicum.de/)).
- **Christian text:** "When you give alms, do not let your left hand know what your right hand
  is doing, so that your alms may be in secret" ([Matthew 6:1-4](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+6%3A1-4&version=RSVCE)).
- **Honest verdict: does not map, and the game should not pretend it does.** Q 2:271 itself
  ranks secret giving *higher* than public — the Quran is internally closer to Matthew 6 than
  a lazy reading suggests. The lived divergence is that zakat is *compulsory calculation*, not
  spontaneous mercy; but a compulsory poor-tax is not any of the Seven, and medieval Christians
  themselves tithed under canon and royal sanction. What the narrator may *observe* — without
  a sin-tag — is only the *heart-posture* difference the Lord taught: alms as the right hand
  forgetting itself, versus the pillar performed. That is Doctrinal Error at most, and only
  barely: treat it as a **doctrinal-observation entry** with no capital-sin tag, and let the
  text say plainly: "Herein we find no new trespass to charge, but a house whose roof and ours
  touch, though the stairs within go down different ways."
- **Narrator logic (the correct excessive-restraint sample):** "In this we find no new charge:
  they alms in secret as well as we, and teach it. We write it, and pass on — for the
  chronicler who findeth sins where none stand is himself in one."

## 8. Surfaced Additions from the Research Pass

Two further practiced items surfaced with defensible mappings:

### 8.1 Ghazi plunder culture (Greed)

- **Islamic text:** "Know that whatever spoils you take, a fifth of it is for Allah and for the
  Messenger" ([Q 8:41](https://corpuscoranicum.de/)) — plunder institutionalized, taxed, and
  sacralized by statute (*ghanima*); and the prophet's own attestation: "The booty has been
  made lawful for me, and was not made so for anyone [of the prophets] before me" ([Sahih
  al-Bukhari 438, verified this pass](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:438)). The *ghazi* ideal —
  frontier raiding as sanctified livelihood — is the social form; Iqta' fief and booty-dole
  were its economy through our period.
- **Christian counter:** "You shall not covet…" ([Exodus 20:17](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Exodus+20%3A17&version=RSVCE));
  contentment: "Keep your life free from love of money" ([Hebrews 13:5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Hebrews+13%3A5&version=RSVCE));
  and the prophetic woe against plunder regimens: "Woe to those who join house to house, who
  add field to field, until there is no more room"
  ([Isaiah 5:8](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Isaiah+5%3A8&version=RSVCE)).
- **Sin-mapping: Greed (defensible).** A theology that makes spoils *the prophet's distinctive
  lawful mark* is covetousness given a seal. But mirror hard: the Latin sack-culture of the
  First Crusade ([Vol. I §4.8]) and crusader plunder at Lisbon, Jerusalem, and Constantinople
  1204 are the same sin in Latin; the narrator names Louis's and Bohemond's bootees before
  Saladin's ghazis.
- **Narrator logic:** "His prophet's mark among the prophets was the booty licensed; and his
  best rider is the man who carrieth off the peerless steed and sacramentheth the avenue by
  which he came. We too have sacked cities in the Name; the difference is: we blush for it in
  our chronicles, and he canons it in his."

### 8.2 Qisas and the blood-money calculus (Wrath, partial)

- **Islamic text:** "And there is life for you in retaliation, O men of understanding"
  ([Q 2:178-179](https://corpuscoranicum.de/)); the practice added *diyya* — blood-price
  payable by the killer's kin, tariffed (famously: a woman's diyya at half a man's in the
  classical schools; a dhimmi's at fractions in several). Retaliation as a *regulated legal
  estate*, with the deep-mirrored oddity that blood can be priced.
- **Christian counter:** "You have heard that it was said, 'An eye for an eye'… But I say to
  you, Do not resist one who is evil. But if any one strikes you on the right cheek, turn to
  him the other also… Love your enemies" ([Matthew 5:38-44](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew+5%3A38-44&version=RSVCE));
  "Beloved, never avenge yourselves, but leave it to the wrath of God"
  ([Romans 12:19](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Romans+12%3A19&version=RSVCE)).
- **Sin-mapping: Wrath (partial, defensible).** Retaliation *limited* is an improvement over
  feud; but Christ abolishes the *license itself* — the sermon does not tariff the eye, it
  refuses it. Map Wrath only at the *systematicization of vengeance* (the cycling economy),
  and couple every such text with the mirror: Latin vengeance-feuds and crusader reprisals
  (Kerak 1183, the Counter-merchant massacres) are the same estate, uncodified.
- **Narrator logic:** "In their book is a price-list for blood; the Lord's Book hath no column
  for what vengeance oweth, for it saith: the Lord shall repay."

## 9. Design Notes: Writer's Reference Table (mirror of Vol. I §4)

> Voice model unchanged: the sober Brother Chronicler circa 1185, William of Tyre become devout.
> Latin cadence in English dress; measured; judgment-bearing; grief under the judgment. The
> mirror rule from Vol. I §4.8 stands: every campaign must rebuke the Latin parallel.

### 9.1 Dhimma/Jizya
- **Sin tag:** Pride (institutional) + Oppression tag. **Obstinacy note:** marks sin-depth-3
  qadi/commander cadre *flavor*, never a stat — Obstinacy tracks entrenchment of the *soul*,
  not office. But the *systemic* encounter (a jizya-collection scene on a side-map) may carry
  higher narrated weight than its tier suggests; write the collector as a functionary trapped
  in a wicked office, with a path to conversion.
- **Flavor sample:** *"They have writ a tariff for the stranger's faith, and made his bent neck
  the receipt that payment is true. 'Woe to them that decree iniquitous decrees,' saith Isaiah
  — and this is a decree with a seal."*
- **Banned register:** treating dhimmis as "collaborators" or billing the *humiliated* as
  culpable; Muslim-stereotype sneer at the payer of jizya; any boisterous tax-collector
  characterization that reads the *person* instead of the *statute*.

### 9.2 Apostasy law
- **Sin tag:** Wrath (institutional). **Obstinacy note:** the mechanic consequence is on the
  *convert's* side: a baptized archetype post-conversion should read as a marked man in flavor;
  Hagiography's Testimonies page may specifically grieve "his house is made a prey unto his
  father's law." This *deepens* the stakes of CONVERSION.md §3 success. Consider a scripted
  campaign node where a new convert is sheltered — the game's sanctuary fiction tropes are
  canon-legal here.
- **Flavor sample:** *"Our Lord asked the Twelve, 'Will ye also go away?' — and the air stood
  still and no sword answered. Their book answereth with the sword: 'whoso changeth his
  religion, slay him.' Behold the difference between the Shepherd and the keeper of a gaol."*
- **Banned register:** implying the jail-keeper is hateful *as a Muslim* — he is hateful as a
  *statute*-enforcer; bloodlust staging at apostasy-trial scenes; "they kill their own" as a
  crowd jeer.

### 9.3 Slavery & concubinage
- **Sin tag:** Greed + Lust (twinned, institutional). **Obstinacy note:** captives encountered
  are *victims*, not foes of higher tier; if archetype tables ever include a "slave-soldier"
  (mamluk-recruit tier-0 conscript), his Obstinacy should run *low* (see CONVERSION.md §6
  tier-0) and his sin-depth 0 — he is conscripted flesh.
- **Flavor sample:** *"He weighed a man's walking years against coin in the market of Aleppo,
  and the same evening found it written in his book that Allah permitteth the right hand its
  possessings. We sell captives too — and the chronicler's pen doth blot and falter in writing
  it, for it is our shame."*
- **Banned register:** any harem-visual or slave-market staging that even glances at
  titillation; "their women are chattel therefore contemptible"; making freedmen grateful
  props for our piety.

### 9.4 Women under fiqh
- **Sin tag:** Lust (child-bride precedent & concubine estate) + Wrath (Q 4:34 clause) +
  Pride-with-Oppression (halved witness/inheritance). **Obstinacy note:** a learned female of
  the adversary camp is not an auto-tier-3; her entrenchment is independent of the law over
  her. Boss-flavor only: a *qaḍī-al-quḍāt*-class villain may be flavored as *the lawyer of the
  halves* — his crime is the *statute*, not weight of woman.
- **Flavor sample:** *"Their law giveth the daughter half a son, maketh two women one man
  before the judge, and putteth a rod in the husband's hand against the disobedient wife; and
  in the lifetime of their prophet, a child of nine was brought to his bed, and the ink of the
  book saith it was lawful. 'Husbands, love your wives as Christ loved the Church' — the two
  sentences do not dwell in the same heaven."*
- **Banned register:** the whole forbidden quiver of Vol. I §4.5 (arena-titillation, women as
  loot-jokes); also banned: using Muslim women as rhetorical props for how *enlightened* we
  are — Latin canon law's own tendernesses were thin enough.

### 9.5 Oath-expiation & hudna
- **Sin tag:** Doctrinal Error (Untruth) + Wrath hook at operational breach (hudna-as-ambush).
  Extends Vol. I §1.6; do not restate taqiyya. **Obstinacy note:** truce-breaking commander
  archetypes may carry *narrative* sin-depth-2 flag; the engine tier stays a *personal* marker,
  not a legal one.
- **Flavor sample:** *"They have set the broken oath a price in loaves. The Lord said: let your
  Yes be Yes — hath no ledger, no clause. And yet it was Raynald who broke the truce at Kerak
  and brought ruin upon us all; Scripture suffereth not his name to be forgot, for the Latin
  perjurer is as stinking in the record as the Saracen licensed."*
- **Banned register:** "they always lie" truce-meter mechanics; Perfidious-foe *character*
  flags (only *event* flags — this truce, this breach); sneering at expiation as a "cheat
  code" (Vol. I's register).

### 9.6 Fatalism as piety
- **Sin tag:** Doctrinal Error (no mapping); strict, rare Sloth at *character-abuse* level
  only. **Obstinacy note:** do not confound fatalism with Obstinacy mechanics; they are
  unrelated quantities (Obstinacy is *willful* entrenchment).
- **Flavor sample:** *"Their book saith the pen is dry; and the idle among their folk make a
  pillow of the drying. Yet their wiser own — Ghazali no less — thrusteth the pillow from them.
  Judge ye the pillow, not the sleeper."*
- **Banned register:** "they're all fatalists therefore passive" gameplay; inshallah-as-joke;
  turning the doctrine into a debuff-stat on adversary AI.

### 9.7 Zakat (unmappable)
- **Sin tag:** none. **Obstinacy note:** no mechanical consequence.
- **Flavor sample (the whole entry, kept short by design):** *"Here we find no charge. Their
  book also teacheth the secret gift is better. We note the nearness and pass on."*
- **Banned register:** any attempt to make almsgiving a point against them; framing zakat as
  "buying heaven" against Vol. I §4.3's own ban.

### 9.8 Ghazi plunder
- **Sin tag:** Greed (defensible). **Boss-flavor:** a tier-2 ghazi-emir boss (CONVERSION.md §6
  tier-2, "ghazi culture") narratively *exemplifies* Greed-doctrine; his visible corruption at
  tier-2 may be flavored as *avarice rendered in the bearing*. His conversion beat pays
  from pennons to shoulders: a rich war-gear laid down at an altar.
- **Flavor sample:** *"Their booty is a fifth for the prophet's house, and the four-fifths
  holy. The Lord's tithing goeth all the other way — from the hand, not into it. We have had
  our sackers of cities, and boasted of their crowns; the chronicler confesseth it, for the
  sin made Catholic is not therefore made sweet."*
- **Banned register:** using "looter" as a per-ethnicity bark; erasing Latin sack-culture from
  campaign texts (Vol. I §4.8).

### 9.9 Summary matrix

| # | Practiced item | Sin tag | Mappable? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dhimma/jizya humiliation | Pride + Oppression | Yes (institutional) |
| 2 | Apostasy law | Wrath | Yes |
| 3 | Slavery & concubinage | Greed + Lust | Yes |
| 4 | Women under fiqh | Lust + Wrath + Pride-with-Oppression | Yes (compounded) |
| 5 | Oath-expiation & hudna | Doctrinal Error + Wrath hook | Partial |
| 6 | Fatalism/qadar | Doctrinal Error (Sloth, rare, character-level) | Mostly no |
| 7 | Zakat/publicity | none | No — explicitly unmappable |
| 8a | Ghazi plunder | Greed | Yes |
| 8b | Qisas/diyya | Wrath (partial) | Partial |

**Where the analysis lands vs. Vol. I:** practiced doctrine maps *better* to the Seven than
abstract doctrine did — dhimma humiliation is Pride-with-a-seal, apostasy-death is
Wrath-with-a-seal, the right-hand-possessions statute is Greed-and-Lust-with-a-seal. The
common phrase "with a seal" is the engine note: the practiced sins are doctrinally *carried*,
so their Obstinacy contribution belongs at the *archetype's narrative tier* level
(CONVERSION.md §6), not at personal per-instance grudges. The mirror rule hardens here in one
more place: nearly every practiced sin has a Latin parallel the narrator must name, and Vol. II
must therefore be read *with* Vol. I §4.8 in every campaign.
